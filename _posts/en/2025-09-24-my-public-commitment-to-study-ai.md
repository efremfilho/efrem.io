One of my goals by the end of the year is to become more of a specialist in AI. My first attempt was "I'll create a gem/project that will be my tutor". It didn't go that well.

After poking around a bit, I landed on Stanford's free videos, and from what I'm reading/seeing, they're still one of the best references.

I plan to watch:

- machine learning (cs229) - both of them
- deep learning and deep learning for computer vision
- LLMs
- Transformers
- Agentic AI
- Reinforcement learning
- Deep generative models

And in parallel, I'll watch statistical learning with Python, which has shorter classes.

Has anyone watched them? Are they worth it? I plan to report as I go and create a NotebookLM — split by topic, I think.

This is so I have a public commitment, and some public shaming if I don't do it. Let's see if I learn something :)
