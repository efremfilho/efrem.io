It's impressive how GenAI is making me — I hope — a better teacher. Here's what I do today:

- I generate "deep researches" until I feel comfortable with the topic
- I take the references, the notes and the research I used and create a NotebookLM to share
- I generate class notes using Gemini to organize the research and put it in a better order
- I record the audio of the class
- I upload the audio to the NotebookLM
- I take the transcript and feed it to Gemini to summarize the class and share it afterwards

I do this last part because of the examples, the concepts that weren't clear, and to have a deliverable.

It's been an incredible experience. Just one detail: did I ever say it reduced the time I take? On the contrary, it even increased, but I think it improved the QUALITY absurdly.

It stopped being a snapshot and now has deliverables that can be used for much longer. \o/
