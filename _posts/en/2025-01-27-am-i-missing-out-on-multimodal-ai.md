At the company kickoff I got to use a little text-to-video, but it made me wonder a lot about how to use it in other cases.

After a few years using #genai, I still only manage to use text-to-text professionally, and basically in automations. None of the multimodal ones. Maybe something with voice at some point.

My focus has always been B2B, so I don't know the B2C world very well. Maybe I missed something there.

Just as I focused on a single foundation model provider, and on automation instead of chat, I also focused on a single modality. Now I'm starting to think it's worth exploring other modalities.

If anyone has a cool case, shall we swap notes? :)
