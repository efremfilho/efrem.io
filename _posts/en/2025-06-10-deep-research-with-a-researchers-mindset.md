I've been using deep research more lately (ChatGPT and Gemini) and I'm... VERY impressed. Things changed a lot when I started thinking with a "researcher's" mindset.

- I define hypotheses
- I describe the goal
- I give context
- I review the research execution plan

I've already derived one research from another (and followed the same steps). Both invalidated my hypotheses 🤡 but elegantly, and bringing the points.

There's still a problem with the sources they bring, but in ChatGPT I managed to steer it well. One of the tests was that it could only use "peer-reviewed" articles with a high impact factor... and it complied. However, I don't know how the models indexed articles behind paywalls... I guess they do what most researchers do.

By the way, all the researches did a better job than I do when researching.
