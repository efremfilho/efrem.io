Anyone else using the term "context engineering"? I really like the principle, but I kept calling it prompting techniques + tools.

I still wonder whether we need yet another concept. BUUUT I like the idea that the most important human skill right now is knowing how to communicate, and how to give agents all the information and tools they need to do their tasks.

If you want to learn more about the concept: https://blog.langchain.com/the-rise-of-context-engineering/
