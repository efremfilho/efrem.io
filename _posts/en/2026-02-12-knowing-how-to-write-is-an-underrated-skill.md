With these more powerful GenAI models, I feel more and more that an underrated skill is knowing how to write. Everyone I know who is doing well "writes" well.

I don't mean it in the literary sense; I mean knowing how to express yourself, ask for things and organize your ideas. Skills help minimize that need, but I still see a big difference between those who stop, think and ask and those who keep asking "iteratively" while vibing...

If you want a tip: learn to write well so you can ask AI agents for things.
