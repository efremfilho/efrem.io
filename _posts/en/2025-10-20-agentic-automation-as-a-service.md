I managed to sketch the first design of a "service as a product" offering, similar to Designjoy. It would be 10k a month for about one person, plus me guaranteeing the delivery. However, the number of people matters less. Let me explain.

I talked to Unieuro, where I teach in the technology programs. We locked in the proposal. Of course, we can still take on a more tailor-made project.

The idea is to guarantee AI automations for the company. Instead of having a team or a person for that, you have us. Total focus on delivery, not on billable hours. Fewer video calls and more delivery.

Don't need much this month? Pause it. Need a lot? No problem — same price, with one automation coming in at a time. I want to see if I can create something for agentic automations in this model. Designjoy already did this for design; the idea is for us to be "agentic automation as a service".

In the past I ran some tests with PM3. Thanks, Marcell Almeida, for the partnership, as always! Now it's about having a pool of companies to refine this, so it's not a side project in my life but the main thing, mixed with my academic life. \o/

Anyone up for testing it and helping build it? If you want to know more, just ping me :)
