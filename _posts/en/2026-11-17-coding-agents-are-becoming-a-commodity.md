Over the last few months, I've been testing switching from Claude Code to Codex (which I used to hate) and Gemini (Antigravity CLI). I think I've reached a conclusion: coding agents have become (or are becoming) a commodity.

What I needed to do: switch to agents.md, create a /docs folder to record decisions and learnings, and finally, adapt to the CLI/web environments. I haven't tested installed apps (beyond the CLI). With that in place, I'm liking different styles from each one. Claude Code is the most everyday one. Codex is faster and more proactive — I often prefer it for something risky. Gemini has been the most thorough in reviews, believe it or not.

Oh, another behavior I've changed: I'm talking more with agents (shoutout, Arthur Castro!). BUUUUT I think there are moments when I want to talk — brainstorming — and moments when I'd rather stop and write — especially in reviews. I think I'm finding my multimodal process.

I think I'll keep using all three, and both talking and typing. :)
