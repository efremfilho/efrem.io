*Commenting on a post by Simon Lafortune arguing that PMs are the most valuable people in the AI era.*

Glad I'm not the only one. The thing I disagree with is that it's not necessarily a product person, but anyone who has critical thinking and knows how to use AI.

BUUUT I totally get the argument for product-minded people — PMs, designers, (product-minded) devs — knowing the reasoning behind a product (life cycle, UX, validation, etc.) is still very much worth it.
