Thinking out loud here: which of the big tech consultancies will be the first to talk about federation in this vibe coding world?

I saw this happen with microservices and with data — yes, there are others, but I didn't see them as much. You only have monoliths, we break them into several services/data sets, and now the idea is no longer to put them back together, but someone needs to organize this mess. That's where federation comes in.

Maybe what's missing in the "vibe coding" world is federation, so it gets accepted in the big corporations out there.

I don't know, just thinking out loud. The good thing is we already have plenty of lessons learned.
