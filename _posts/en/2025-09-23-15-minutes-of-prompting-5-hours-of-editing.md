Yesterday I was talking about the AIs' research modes. Then I looked at how long it took me to get something done. I spent 15 minutes writing the prompt. 18 pages of analysis. I spent 5 hours editing.

It's not just about speed, but about being able to unblock things and take what you deliver to another level. The responsibility for what was delivered is still mine, not "the AI's".

Still, I wouldn't deliver 18 pages in one day with everything I wanted. The work is STILL hard — for now. BUT now I feel I have something to unblock me faster :)
