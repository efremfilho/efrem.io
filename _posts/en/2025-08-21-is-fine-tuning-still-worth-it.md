I built a "fine-tuned" model at OLX about 3 years ago. Foundation models have evolved a lot, and so have RAG and other "tools". As far as I know, fine-tuning a model is no longer worth it.

I'm writing this because I want to hear other people's experiences. And what I've read out there confirms what I'm seeing. I think using other techniques is much more interesting.

Some texts on the subject:

- https://lnkd.in/gk8x3jkF
- https://lnkd.in/gUUApt5E

I really like the phrase "fine-tuning isn't injecting knowledge, it's overwriting".
