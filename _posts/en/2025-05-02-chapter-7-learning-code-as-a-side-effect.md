Chapter 7 - what I'm observing about "vibe coding". I often say I don't want to be a more technical person, BUUUT I see that I end up getting more interested in the code, and I think it will end up happening naturally.

I'm bringing this up here because I talk with Giampaolo Lepore about how I don't like this idea of everyone thinking they're a dev. However, I see the same already happens with product and design.

I don't want you to get me wrong. It's still getting clearer in my head. That's normal with new things. :)

- What do I want? To get stronger in product and know how to ask AI well to develop for me.
- What's happening? I'm ending up learning more about code, but as a side effect.

With that, I keep using and doing everything inside Lovable + Supabase + ChatGPT (o3) to get unstuck when Lovable gets stuck. :)
