Two new behaviors I see in my use of GenAI.

- Most of the time I try to solve everything in one prompt. I think it comes largely from creating prompts for agents and automations. So I rarely use it for dialogue in the "chat". Less use as an "oracle" and more use as a tool. I think it's only in the code generators that I still work more iteratively.
- Creating MCP Servers in Zapier and in Pipefy iPaaS makes it clear how they're going to become a standard. I no longer wire up tools, at least during my tests. We'll see in live use. Is it a cannon to solve a small problem? Yes.

GenAI — for now — is less a game of efficiency and more one of effectiveness.
