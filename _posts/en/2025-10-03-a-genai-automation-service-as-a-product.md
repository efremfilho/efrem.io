I'm fixated on the idea of building a "service as a product" focused on (GenAI) automation, on Pipefy and open-source automation tools. One subscription, unlimited automations.

I admire how Designjoy works. No calls, a focus on productivity and asynchronous communication. No-surprise pricing. DJ matched its price to a junior designer in the US. I'm thinking of doing the same, matching it to a solutions engineer.

I don't know, I'm thinking out loud here. Curious to know whether it would work in Brazil and for automations.

There's a product hypothesis for me to test :)
