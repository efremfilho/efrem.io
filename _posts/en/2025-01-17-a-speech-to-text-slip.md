Always being upfront about the limitations of foundation models: I use ChatGPT's speech-to-text a lot. In general, it transcribes about 90% correctly. I like it because I end up giving more context, since I "write more".

However, this was the first time I saw it slip up and reveal where its training came from lol.

#GenAI is good, but you need to understand its limitations.
