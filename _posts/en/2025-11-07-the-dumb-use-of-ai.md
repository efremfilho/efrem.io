I think what tires me most these days is the "dumb" use of AI. People ask AI to do something, send it to others and don't bother to review it! In any field, I see zero problem in using AI to do the writing work, but remember: you're still responsible for the delivery.

Review it, ask for changes, make it YOURS, not something generic and full of references that make no sense at all. Even more so if you delete the references!

It was just a rant. Please don't be that person.
Especially if I'm the person on the other side.
