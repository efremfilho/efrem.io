Man, it borders on RIDICULOUS for YC to launch an RFS for "Cursor for Product Managers" and not MENTION Lovable and the like. I thought... "ah, it must be an old video". It isn't. Then I watched the video. I got even more upset. Let me explain.

OK, I get that it's not about the "build" part, but the discovery part. I thought, "Wait, is a PM's job 'only' discovery?". I gave some explanations of how we use it — as a team, not Éfrem as an individual — to talk to synthetic people, do market analysis, deepen research... BUUUUT I think there's nothing there that an assistant hasn't solved for me. With building it was different.

It really made a difference to use something more than a copilot/assistant AI. In the past I built PMAutomator and abandoned it because I couldn't see what I could add as a differentiator.

Maybe I didn't do good discovery? Must be. Is there something I'm not seeing? VERY likely. And I go back to the beginning... not even mentioning the "builders" that are friendlier to non-devs? I find it strange.

Please tell me I'm wrong and that there's a lot of differentiation in a product like this. :)
