Whoever is saying AgentKit will kill the iPaaS has no idea how enterprises work and hasn't even played with it inside the platform.

I'd be a little worried if I were CrewAI or LangChain. It's (still) totally a tool for devs. Just look at the screenshot of what publishing in the tool looks like. BUUUT even then, companies don't want this tool lock-in. It's the same thing as Microsoft launching Power Platform in the past. Or Google buying AppSheet. A big platform company NEEDS to have "everything".

It was a natural move, and for those in the OpenAI ecosystem, great. Models are becoming a commodity. Just as the cloud did, and AWS, GCP and Azure had to launch a bunch of applications on top to keep the value and the lock-in.

Sensationalism sells. It's the same as when I say here that I hate some tool. lol Like Codex, which I've already trashed, but I'm ready to eat my words on the latest update (I haven't tested it yet out of trauma).

Does anyone remember Opal? The one that was also going to break the vibe coding tools... well, no.

I loved it, and I'll use it more in my trainings because it makes it easier to explain guardrails, set model parameters, show how an agentic process works, etc.

To talk about it, you have to test it. And I've still tested very little, and while sleepy! I'll bring more tests later :)
