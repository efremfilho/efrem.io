Last Friday was my last day at Pipefy. A place that wasn't just a company, but became my last name over the past 2.5 years. What an intense and incredible time! I'm very happy to have taken part in building the product team and the AI strategy.

I'll keep admiring and following what Pipefy has been doing in no-code agent orchestration. Thank you so much for everything! Go crush it out there \o/

As I told Raphael Farinazzo on the Mesa de Produto episode, it had been a long time since I was this excited about my skills as a product person. This was a year for deciding what to focus on. We shut down Zeeplo, I focused on AI, and now I want to focus even more on GenAI.

It's time to dive into GenAI in my studies and in my projects. I'll build some extensions (apps, templates, guides) and implementations of these agentic processes with Pipefy, with other tools and with code! I THINK there are cool things coming.

If you have a cool GenAI project, let's talk :)
